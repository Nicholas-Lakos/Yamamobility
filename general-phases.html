GENERAL PHASES 1–3

Core alignment, dynamic movement, and recovery rhythm.

PHASE 1 · CORE ALIGNMENT — “THE 5 Bs”

Perform in a quiet environment and focus on internal physical sensations.

1. Breath (Taio)

Setup: Stand tall or lie on your back. Use a moderate loop band snugly around the lower rib cage if desired.

Movement: Inhale through the nose and expand the lower ribs outward in 360°. Exhale slowly and let the ribs contract inward while the neck and shoulders stay relaxed.

Goal: 3–5 minutes.

2. Board (Hoko)

Setup: Use a balance pad, stable low block, or floor.

Movement: Balance on one leg. Maintain a tripod foot through the big toe, pinky toe, and heel while slowly shifting forward and backward without dropping the hip.

Goal: 60 seconds per leg with a quiet trunk.

3. Bar (Kankotsu)

Setup: Hold a light PVC pipe, broomstick, or alignment rod across the shoulders.

Movement: With feet and pelvis controlled, slowly rotate the rib cage left and right, then tilt side to side.

Goal: 15 slow, fluid rotations and tilts.

4. Bowl (Tandem)

Setup: Stand on the floor, eyes closed only if safe, with one hand below the navel and one on the lower back.

Movement: Explore tiny anterior/posterior pelvic tilts and small circles while tracking pressure shifts.

Goal: 2–3 minutes finding a comfortable neutral position.

5. Bridge (Sekizui)

Setup: Hands and knees on a mat.

Movement: Create a smooth spinal wave from tailbone through the upper spine, then reverse it. Stay within comfortable ranges.

Goal: 2 minutes of continuous, fluid movement.

PHASE 2 · DYNAMIC SPATIAL & KINETIC RE-EDUCATION

1. Judo Falling Mat Work (Ukemi)

On a proper mat or forgiving surface, begin low and practice controlled backward/side rolls: chin tucked, spine rounded, and no direct impact to the head or joints. Goal: 5–8 smooth rolls each direction. Learn ukemi technique from a qualified instructor before progressing.

2. Handstand Variations (Scapular Lock)

Use a wall handstand, wall walk, or bear position. Push the floor away, keep the shoulder girdle active, and control the ribs. Goal: 30–45 seconds. Use the regression if you cannot maintain control.

3. Featherweight Javelin Throwing

Use a lightweight training implement in a clear throwing area. Turn from the ground up and let the arm follow the body rather than forcing arm speed. Focus on a clean flight path. Goal: 10–15 relaxed throws. Stop with shoulder or elbow pain.

PHASE 3 · RHYTHM & DOWNSHIFT

Use season or moon phase as a personal rhythm cue if you enjoy it, not as a physiological rule; there is not good evidence that lunar phase determines fluid retention, autonomic arousal, or joint lubrication.

1. Choose the Rhythm

For a calming session, emphasize a longer, easy exhale. If you want a more alert finish, use even, comfortable breathing rather than forceful breaths.

2. Final Synchronization Flow

Lie on your back in silence for 5 minutes. Breathe diaphragmatically and scan from head to toe, allowing unnecessary muscular tension to soften.
