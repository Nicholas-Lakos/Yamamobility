FLECHA THROWING

Pitcher-focused sequence, momentum & feel

MONDAY — High Intensity & Sequence Loading

Primary throwing day. Focus on generating maximum rotational force from your lower half and delaying your arm.

Warm-up Flips — 5 reps · 15–20 feet

Cue: Stand tall, isolate your wrist/forearm, and flip the Flecha cleanly into a net or turf. Ensure the nose does not dip.

Kneeling / Half-Kneeling Throws — 5 reps

Cue: Keep your lower body pinned. Rotate your chest away, pull cleanly through your chest, and let the Flecha whip through late.

Abbreviated / Rocker Throws — 4 reps

Cue: Start with your front leg planted. Rock your weight onto your back hip, drive the pelvis open, and delay the arm until the absolute last millisecond.

Full Standing Distance Throws — 5 reps

Cue: Throw for distance over grass (90–150+ feet). Try to get a high, clean trajectory without letting the javelin wobble or flutter.

WEDNESDAY — Momentum & Speed Transfer

Use footwork to carry linear speed into your throw, simulating the momentum you generate out of a baseball windup or stretch.

Walking / Dynamic Flips — 5 reps

Cue: Take smooth, slow walking steps forward and release the Flecha with perfect horizontal stability.

One-Step / Step-Behind Throws — 5 reps

Cue: Step your back foot behind your lead foot (like an outfield crow-hop), plant hard on a stiff front leg, and transfer that energy up through your torso.

Crow-Hop Max Velocity Throws — 3 reps

Cue: Take 2–3 aggressive running steps on a line. Explode through the release point while maintaining a pristine, "out-front" baseball release.

FRIDAY — Touch, Feel & Mechanical Consistency

Low-volume, high-focus day designed to lock in your release point before weekend bullpens or games.

Target Flips — 5 reps · 20 feet

Cue: Pick a literal brick or specific square on a net. Throw with zero wobble and 100% precision.

Rocker Throws — 4 reps · 60–70% effort

Cue: Smooth, easy tempo. Do not push the throw with your hand; focus entirely on the feeling of your chest pulling the arm through.

Baseball-to-Flecha Mapping — 2 alternating sets × 3 reps

Cue: Throw 3 reps with the Flecha, immediately pick up a standard 5 oz baseball, and mimic the exact same body-driven feeling for 3 throws into a net.
